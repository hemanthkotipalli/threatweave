# RBI Warning: Threats of Immediate Account Freezing for Non-Compliance with KYC

## Warning from Reserve Bank of India
The RBI strongly advises bank customers not to respond to unsolicited calls, SMS, or emails claiming that their bank account, debit card, or mobile banking service will be frozen immediately due to non-updated KYC (Know Your Customer) records.

## Scam Characteristics & Indicators
1. **Arbitrary Deadlines**: Threats of immediate suspension within 12 or 24 hours inducing panic.
2. **Phishing Links**: Messages contain hyperlinks directing customers to unverified third-party websites mimicking official bank portals.
3. **Demands for Sensitive Data**: The fake website demands sensitive information such as net banking username, password, debit card PIN, and OTP.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `urgency_language`, `credential_request`, `lookalike_domain`.

## Bank Policy Reminder
Banks never require customers to update KYC details through third-party web links or unofficial mobile numbers. KYC updates must be conducted via official banking branches, secured mobile apps, or designated video KYC portals.
