# RBI Digital Banking Directive: Strict Prohibition Against Sharing One-Time Passwords

## Mandatory Safety Protocol
The Reserve Bank of India reiterates that under no circumstances should bank customers share their sensitive authentication credentials with any person, whether online, over the phone, or in person.

## Essential Security Directives
1. **Absolute Confidentiality**: Never share your One-Time Password (OTP), UPI PIN, Net Banking Password, Card Verification Value (CVV), or ATM PIN with anyone, including bank staff.
2. **Zero Verification Request**: Regulated entities, law enforcement agencies, and RBI officials will never contact customers asking for passwords or OTPs to resolve issues or verify identity.
3. **Immediate Reporting**: If credentials are compromised, immediately contact your bank to freeze digital access and register a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `credential_request`, `vishing_script_pattern`.

## Regulatory Protection
Reporting unauthorized electronic banking transactions within three working days provides customers with substantial liability protection under the RBI customer liability framework.
