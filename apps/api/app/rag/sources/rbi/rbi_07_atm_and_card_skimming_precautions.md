# RBI Security Alert: Detection of Physical Card Skimming Devices and Keypad Overlays

## Security Guidelines
The Reserve Bank of India advises consumers on the physical security precautions necessary when using Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) and Point-of-Sale (POS) card terminals.

## Skimming Tactics
1. **Skimmer Installation**: Fraudsters attach covert magnetic stripe readers onto ATM card reader slots to clone card data.
2. **Keypad Overlays and Pinhole Cameras**: Counterfeit keypad overlays and hidden pinhole cameras capture the customer's PIN as it is entered.
3. **Unattended ATM Vestibules**: Skimming devices are most frequently installed in poorly lit, unmonitored standalone ATM kiosks.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `card_skimming`, `credential_request`.

## Preventive Measures
Cover the keypad with your hand when entering your PIN. Inspect the card insertion slot for loose parts or unusual bulges before inserting your debit card.
