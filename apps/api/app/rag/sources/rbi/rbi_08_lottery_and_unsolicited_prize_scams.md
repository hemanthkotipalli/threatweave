# RBI Cautionary Notice: Unsolicited Foreign Remittance and Lottery Win Notifications

## Notice to Public
The Reserve Bank of India alerts the public that it does not maintain accounts of individual citizens, nor does it issue certificates for funds clearance or lottery payouts.

## Typical Scam Lure
1. **Bogus Prize Notification**: Fraudsters send letters, emails, or WhatsApp messages claiming the recipient has won an international lottery or foreign remittance of millions of dollars.
2. **Demands for Advance Fees**: Victims are told that to release the funds, they must pay 'customs duty', 'conversion charges', or 'RBI clearance certificates' into personal bank accounts.
3. **Counterfeit RBI Documentation**: Letters feature forged signatures of RBI governors and fraudulent RBI seals.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `advance_fee_lure`, `urgency_language`, `brand_impersonation`.

## Strict Reminder
RBI does not participate in any commercial transactions with the public. Do not remit any money in response to unsolicited offers of large prizes.
