# RBI Warning: Predatory Micro-Lending Applications and Harassment Tactics

## Public Caution Notice
The Reserve Bank of India warns the public against availing loans through unauthorized and unregistered instant loan mobile applications operating without a valid Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) license.

## Fraudulent Practices & Threats
1. **Exorbitant Deductions**: Apps disburse only a fraction of the sanctioned loan amount, deducting up to 50% as upfront processing fees.
2. **Intrusive Permission Requests**: Apps mandate access to contacts, photo gallery, location, and call logs as a condition of loan approval.
3. **Blackmail and Defamation**: Scammers use harvested contact lists to circulate defamatory messages and morph photos to extort exorbitant interest payments.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `blackmail_lure`, `urgency_language`, `advance_fee_lure`.

## Safe Borrowing Guidelines
Borrowers must verify that any digital lending platform is backed by a registered NBFC or scheduled commercial bank listed on the official RBI website.
