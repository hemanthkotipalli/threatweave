# Scam Intel Alert: Identity Theft Exploiting Aadhaar Enabled Payment Systems (AePS)

## Threat Intelligence Briefing
Organized criminal rings are exploiting identity theft and cloned biometric markers to conduct unauthorized withdrawals via the Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS) from rural and semi-urban bank accounts.

## Modus Operandi & Indicators
1. **Land Registry and Phishing Harvests**: Scammers obtain Aadhaar numbers and biometric impressions from compromised land registry documents or deceptive phishing portals.
2. **Silica Clone Creation**: Cloned silicone fingerprints are used on POS micro-ATMs to initiate merchant cash-out transactions without victim knowledge.
3. **Phishing Pretext**: Attackers circulate links claiming users must verify their biometric status or risk losing social welfare entitlements.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `credential_request`, `lookalike_domain`, `unauthorized_debit`.

## Recommended Safeguards
Citizens are advised to utilize the official mAadhaar application or UIDAI portal to lock their biometrics, unlocking them only when deliberately performing a biometric transaction.
