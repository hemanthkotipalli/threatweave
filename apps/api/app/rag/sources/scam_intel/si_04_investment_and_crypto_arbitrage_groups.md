# Scam Intel Alert: High-Yield Investment Fraud Operating via Closed Messaging Groups

## Threat Intelligence Analysis
Cybercrime syndicates are orchestrating investment and institutional stock trading fraud using sponsored social media ads to funnel victims into closed WhatsApp and Telegram channels.

## Execution Pattern
1. **Elite Mentor Persona**: Scammers pose as certified market analysts sharing stock tips, accompanied by fake group members posting fabricated screenshots of massive daily returns.
2. **Custom Trading App**: Victims are instructed to download specialized APKs or register on custom web dashboards displaying fictitious trading balances.
3. **Withdrawal Freeze**: When the victim attempts to withdraw their initial deposit or profits, the platform freezes withdrawals, demanding tax payments or margin top-ups.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `investment_lure`, `fake_payment_confirmation`, `urgency_language`, `advance_fee_lure`.

## Defensive Rules
Trade exclusively through SEBI-registered stockbrokers. Any platform promising guaranteed double-digit daily or weekly returns is fraudulent.
