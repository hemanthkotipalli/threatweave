# Scam Intel Alert: Impersonation of Customs Officials Regarding Seized Illegal Parcels

## Threat Intelligence Summary
A recurring social engineering pattern involves outbound calls claiming that an international parcel consigned by the victim has been intercepted at airport customs containing narcotics, expired passports, or illegal currency.

## Attack Sequence
1. **Automated Robocall**: Call starts with an IVR alert: 'Your parcel has been detained by Customs. Press 9 to speak with our verification desk.'
2. **Fear Escalation**: The fraudster claims the parcel was shipped to Taiwan or Malaysia containing MDMA or unauthorized documents, threatening immediate registration of an FIR.
3. **Demand for Clearance Deposit**: Scammers offer to provide a 'clearance certificate' if the victim deposits a refundable clearance deposit into a designated account.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `vishing_script_pattern`, `urgency_language`, `authority_impersonation`, `advance_fee_lure`.

## Verification Protocol
Customs authorities do not contact citizens via WhatsApp or automated phone calls demanding financial deposits to dismiss narcotics allegations. Disconnect and report immediately.
