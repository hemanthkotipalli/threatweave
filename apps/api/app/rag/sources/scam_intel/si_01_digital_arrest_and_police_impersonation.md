# Scam Intel Alert: Digital Arrest Scripts Coercing Victims via Video Law Enforcement Calls

## Threat Intelligence Summary
Law enforcement agencies and cyber threat intelligence units have issued urgent alerts regarding 'digital arrest' scams. Sophisticated criminal syndicates coerce victims into days of continuous video isolation, extorting life savings under threat of prosecution.

## Script Architecture & Modus Operandi
1. **Initial Threat**: The victim receives an automated call claiming their Aadhaar or phone number was used in a major crime (e.g., money laundering or 16 forged passports sent via FedEx).
2. **Official Video Setting**: The call is transferred to a fake police officer or CBI investigator wearing realistic uniforms in front of police logos and national emblems.
3. **Digital Arrest Coercion**: The fraudster informs the victim they are under 'digital arrest' and forbidden from leaving the camera frame or contacting family, under threat of immediate physical arrest.
4. **Asset Verification Transfer**: The victim is instructed to transfer their entire savings to 'Supreme Court safe custody' or 'RBI verification accounts' which are promised to be returned within 2 hours.
5. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `vishing_script_pattern`, `urgency_language`, `authority_impersonation`, `credential_request`.

## Countermeasure Guidance
Indian law does not recognize 'digital arrest'. Police, CBI, ED, and customs officials never issue arrest orders over video calls or demand money transfers for verification.
