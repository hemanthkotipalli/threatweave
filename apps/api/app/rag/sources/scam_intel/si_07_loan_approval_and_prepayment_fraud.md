# Scam Intel Alert: Pre-Approved Personal Loan Scams Demanding Security Deposits

## Threat Intelligence Analysis
Unsolicited messages distributed via SMS and WhatsApp offer pre-approved personal loans of INR 2,00,000 to INR 10,00,000 with zero documentation and ultra-low interest rates (e.g. 2% per annum).

## Modus Operandi
1. **Fake Sanction Letters**: The fraudster transmits forged PDF approval letters displaying logos of reputable financial institutions or the Ministry of Finance.
2. **Advance Processing Demands**: The victim is instructed to deposit 'processing fees', 'NOC charges', or 'refundable insurance premiums' into an individual savings account.
3. **Escalating Fees**: Once paid, scammers invent additional regulatory hurdles (e.g., GST certificate fees) until the victim realizes the deception.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `advance_fee_lure`, `urgency_language`, `brand_impersonation`.

## Borrower Safeguard
Reputable financial institutions never ask borrowers to deposit processing fees into private bank accounts prior to loan disbursement.
