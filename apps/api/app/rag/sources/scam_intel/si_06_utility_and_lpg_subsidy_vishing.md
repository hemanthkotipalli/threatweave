# Scam Intel Alert: Automated IVR Vishing Targeting LPG Gas Cylinder Subsidies

## Threat Intelligence Summary
Widespread robocall campaigns target residential consumers claiming that their subsidized LPG cylinder connection is pending Aadhaar eKYC validation and will be terminated within 24 hours.

## Attack Flow
1. **Robocall Notification**: Automated voice prompts customer that government subsidy credits have failed due to bank mapping errors.
2. **Call Transfer to Operator**: Victim presses 1 and is connected to a scammer posing as a distribution officer.
3. **Collection of Banking Identifiers**: The scammer requests full bank account numbers, debit card details, and SMS OTP under the pretext of subsidy re-linking.
4. **Relevant Threat Indicators**: `vishing_script_pattern`, `credential_request`, `urgency_language`.

## Consumer Advice
LPG gas distributors do not conduct eKYC or update bank mapping over incoming phone calls. Visit your authorized local distributor agency in person to rectify any billing discrepancies.
